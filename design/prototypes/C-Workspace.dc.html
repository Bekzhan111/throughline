Killing debt: snowball vs. avalanche

There are two popular ways to order your debts. The avalanche pays the highest interest rate first — on paper, it costs you the least. The snowball pays the smallest balance first, so you close accounts quickly and feel progress early.

WHERE THE BOOKS DISAGREE
Ramsey
Quick wins keep you motivated, and motivation is what actually gets debt paid.
The math view
Every month on a high-rate card costs more. Rate first is cheaper over time.
Tie-breaker from Housel: a plan you can stick with beats a perfect plan you abandon.
