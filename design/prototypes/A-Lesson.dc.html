DAY 7 · PERSONAL FINANCE

Pay yourself first

Most budgets fail for the same reason: saving is whatever is left at the end of the month, and usually nothing is left.

Flip the order. Move money to savings the day your paycheck lands, before you can see it in checking.1 Then spend what remains without guilt.
